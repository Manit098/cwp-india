# Code of Conduct

We are committed to keeping this project welcoming, respectful, and inclusive for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate toward others.
- Give helpful, constructive feedback.
- Be open to different viewpoints and ideas.
- Focus on the work rather than personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include, but are not limited to:

- Harassment, insults, or threats
- Hate speech or discriminatory comments
- Trolling, spam, or disruptive behavior
- Sharing private information without permission

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers through the repository's official channels or GitHub contact options.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful comments, block users, or take other actions needed to keep the project safe and productive.
